MY STORY

From Poland, With Love

My love for cooking began in Poland, where the kitchen was always the heart of our home. It was where family gathered, stories were shared, and food was made with love. Those early memories inspired a lifelong passion for creating meals that bring people together.

For more than 17 years, I have been the owner and executive chef of Magdalena’s Creperie in Bellingham, Washington. What began as a dream grew into a place where guests could enjoy handcrafted food, warm hospitality, and the feeling of being welcomed like family.

My culinary journey has also taken me to Brittany, France, where I trained in the traditional art of French crêpe making. Learning from the region where crêpes were born taught me to appreciate simple, beautiful ingredients and the importance of honoring tradition. I continue to refine my skills through advanced culinary training in Florence, Italy, always learning, growing, and finding new inspiration.
